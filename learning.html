03 / MAKE IT STICK

Small habits. Clearer thinking.

01

Close the notes.

After learning something, put the page away. Write down what you remember. Then check the gaps. Retrieving an idea helps you retain it.

02

Explain it simply.

Talk through an idea as if you’re teaching a friend. When you get stuck, you’ve found the part to revisit. Use an example of your own.

03

Come back to it.

Revisit a topic tomorrow, then a few days later. Short sessions spread over time help more than trying to fit everything into one evening.
